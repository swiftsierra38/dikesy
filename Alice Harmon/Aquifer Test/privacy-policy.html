Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Aquifer Test is provided by Alice Harmon (alice.harmon54@gmail.com).

The app works offline for pumping-test field notes. Water-level readings, optional well coordinates you choose to capture, and fit results stay on your device. Location is used only when you tap to compute observation distance r; no map is shown. Local notifications remind you before scheduled readings if you allow them.

On launch the app may read a public remote configuration that can open an HTTPS page in Safari. That configuration does not include your field data. The app does not require an account, does not use tracking, and has no in-app purchases.
