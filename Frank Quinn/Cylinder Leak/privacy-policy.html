Privacy Policy

App: Cylinder Leak
Developer: Frank Quinn
Contact: frank.quinn76@163.com
Effective: 2026-11-16

Cylinder Leak is an offline engine compression and leak-down log for iPhone. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data we process on device. Engine profiles, compression and leak-down readings, listen level numbers, and notes you enter stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload this workshop data to our servers.

Microphone. When you hold LISTEN, the app briefly measures sound level at a selected port. Audio is not recorded or saved. Microphone access is used only for that measurement.

Startup configuration. On launch the app may read a single remote configuration record that can open a web page in Safari. That path does not send your engine readings.

Sharing. If you export a CSV or share a report, the system share sheet is used and you choose the destination.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change.
