Service

Repack Cells is an offline tool for regrouping recovered battery cells into an S-series P-parallel pack on this iPhone. Packs, cell readings, layouts, and exports stay on the device. There is no account and no in-app purchase.

Camera and notifications

Camera access is optional for scanning tester LCD numbers. Notifications are optional reminders for rest-check due dates. You can decline either and continue with manual entry.

Disclaimer

The Application does not certify cell safety, control a welder or BMS, or replace professional battery engineering judgment. Recycled cells can fail later.
