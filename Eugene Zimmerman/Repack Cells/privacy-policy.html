Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Repack Cells app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Eugene Zimmerman (collectively, the "Application"). Eugene Zimmerman is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not collect, log, or store any personal information when you download and use it. Registration is not required. Packs, cell readings, group layouts, rest-check voltages, and exported build sheets stay on this iPhone. Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a tester screen; recognized numbers stay on this device. Local notifications remind you when rest-check readings are due and are scheduled on this device only. When you choose to share a CSV or PNG, the system share sheet sends it only where you pick. It does not require an account and has no in-app purchases. If the Application is used with an active internet connection, technical protocol data (such as your ephemeral IP address) is transmitted to facilitate network connectivity, but this data is not retained or used for tracking.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

No. The Application does not request location access.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Since the Application does not collect any information for the Service Provider, no data is shared with third parties. A share sheet delivers a file only to the destination you choose.

What are my opt-out rights?

Since the Application does not collect personal information through normal use, uninstalling it simply removes the Application from your device.

If you contact the Service Provider directly or voluntarily provide information by other means, you may request deletion of that information by contacting eugene.zimmerman26@sina.com.

Security

Because the Application does not collect personal data, the risk of personal data exposure is minimal. However, no security system is completely secure.
