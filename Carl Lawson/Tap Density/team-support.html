Tap Density is a bench log for powder lots. Densities, the Hausner ratio, and the Carr class are calculated from the mass and volumes you enter and from taps counted on this iPhone. They are a reading aid for the method you already follow. They are not a certified assay and they do not replace your lab procedure.

The app is free. There are no in-app purchases and no account.
