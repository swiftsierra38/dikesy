Tap Density is an offline powder-lot log for iPhone. It records bulk and tapped volumes, counts taps, and shows density, Hausner ratio, and Carr class. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on this iPhone. Lot names, masses, cylinder volumes, tap counts, densities, and notes you enter stay on the device. The app does not upload this bench data to our servers.

Camera. If you attach a photo of a cylinder or lot label, that image is saved with the run on this iPhone. The camera is used only when you choose to attach a photo.

Motion. While a tap run is listening, the app reads the accelerometer to count valid taps. Motion samples are not saved as a track and are not uploaded.

Sharing. If you export a CSV, the system share sheet is used and you choose the destination.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy. The effective date above will change.
