Sheave Face turns four feeler gaps on an adjustable pulley into angular misalignment, parallel offset, a front or rear foot shim, and an axial move. The numbers are a reading aid for the alignment you already do at the machine. They are not a certified survey and they do not replace the maker's procedure.

The app is free. There are no in-app purchases and no account.
