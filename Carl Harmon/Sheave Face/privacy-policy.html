Sheave Face is an offline pulley-face gauge for iPhone. It records four feeler gaps, calculates angular misalignment and parallel offset, and shows a foot-shim and axial-move prescription. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on this iPhone. Drive names, sheave diameters, center distance, foot spacing, gap readings, shim values, and notes you enter stay on the device. The app does not upload this bench data to our servers.

Camera and photo library. If you attach a photo of a sheave face or a feeler set, that image is saved with the reading on this iPhone. The camera and the photo library are used only when you choose to attach a photo.

Sharing. If you export a CSV, the system share sheet is used and you choose the destination.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy. The effective date above will change.
