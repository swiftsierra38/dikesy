Course Stick support

Developer: Henry Tucker
Contact: henry.tucker96@126.com

Course Stick keeps one wall’s courses and joints on this iPhone. Lock the joints that must stay put. Open courses share the height that is left. If a joint falls outside the limits, the stick names how many more courses to unlock.

Set the sill and the lintel. If the opening misses a course line, raise the sill, lower the lintel, or change the joint so both heights land on a course.

A brick photo stays with that wall. Share writes a PDF or CSV through the system share sheet. There is no account and nothing to buy.
