Privacy Policy

App: Course Stick
Developer: Henry Tucker
Contact: henry.tucker96@126.com
Effective: 2026-11-16

Course Stick is an offline brick-course gauge for iPhone. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on this iPhone. Wall names, brick heights, joint limits, course locks, sill and lintel heights, and the brick photo you attach stay on this iPhone. The app does not upload that workshop data.

Camera and photo library. The camera adds a photo of a brick on the wall you are measuring. The photo library attaches a brick photo that is already on this iPhone. Those photos are stored with that wall on this iPhone.

Sharing. If you share a PDF or CSV of the course sheet, the system share sheet is used and you choose the destination.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. We may update this policy. The effective date above will change.
